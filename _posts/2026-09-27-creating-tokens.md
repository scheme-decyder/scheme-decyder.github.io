Most miniatures hangers need dedicated tokens. Official ones are often expensive, and although 3D printable tokens are an option, I don't have access to a multicolour printer, and I don't want to paint each one.

Recently, I've been debating how to represent spells and abilities for my Khymaera Warmachine army. I would like them to be themed to my colour scheme. There are official ones, but I don't want to buy them because they tend to go out of date fairly quickly with game updates. I found a post by "Boom Boom" on the Warmachine Discord where he used coin protectors, and I thought I could adapt that.

Here's a sneak peek of what they look like at the end:
[![Finished Product]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/tokens/final_tokens.jpg)]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/tokens/final_tokens.jpg){:.glightbox}

For this I bought the following (Sorry for the Amazon links but it's the only place I could find them):
* [30mm Coin Protectors](https://www.amazon.com.au/dp/B07PZ7W9QV?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title&th=1) 
* [30mm Circle Punch](https://www.amazon.com.au/dp/B0B7J7GG91?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title&th=1)

The initial overlay is pretty high for an army. However it will get significantly cheaper as you make more. The coin protectors I linked is two cases with 100 tokens each. Probably 3 armies worth! (If you're local to me and want to make these - hit me up to borrow the hole punch).

Make sure you get a coin case with the spacers, this props the paper up against the glass without you having to cut anything out! 

I then designed the [token file](https://github.com/scheme-decyder/resources/blob/main/Tokens.svg). The tokens are 1-2mm bigger than the hole diameter to prevent whitespace on the sides. It should hide it if the hole punch is a bit off. If you want to use it you can edit it in an svg editor like [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org) (it's free!). I'd recommend changing the font to something cool, I used [Firlest](https://www.1001fonts.com/firlest-font.html).

[![Token Sheet]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/tokens/token_sheet.jpg)]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/tokens/token_sheet.jpg){:.glightbox}

I printed the page at officeworks on 200gsm paper to give it a bit more integrity. After that it's a quick process of hole punching each token out and placing it in the coin protector.

[![Punching tokens]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/tokens/cut_out.jpg)]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/tokens/cut_out.jpg){:.glightbox}
[![Adding tokens to protector]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/tokens/add_tokens.jpg)]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/tokens/add_tokens.jpg){:.glightbox}

To line up the punch, cut a strip of tokens and hold the hole punch upside down.

[![Token Aligning]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/tokens/align_token.jpg)]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/tokens/align_token.jpg){:.glightbox}

It even comes with it's own case! I've tried to use pieces of paper to separate abilities for different casters, but eventually I'll look into designing my own case with expandable partitions for easy separation. Alternatively, my friend printed different coloured circles for each caster and placed them on the back of each token, as another way of being able to sort or find tokens easily.

[![Current Token Case]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/tokens/token_case.jpg)]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/tokens/token_case.jpg){:.glightbox}

If any spells or abilities are changed, I can just update the template, print a new sheet, and pop it in any spare or retired token cases!
